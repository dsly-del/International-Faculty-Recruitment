02 · POSITIONS

Position Types

Three faculty appointment tracks are available.

Faculty (Tenure-Track)

A researcher with academic expertise and a track record of research achievements that meet the university's criteria for full-time faculty appointment.

Faculty (Teaching-Focused)

An educator exclusively responsible for teaching major courses and mentoring students, backed by specialized expertise in foreign language education.

Visiting Professor

A visiting educator appointed on a semester basis, primarily responsible for teaching courses designed for international students.

03 · BENEFITS

Benefits for Tenure-Track Faculty

  • SalaryEquivalent to Hanyang University's Tenure-Track faculty salary.
    An additional teaching allowance of up to USD 3,900 per semester is paid when teaching summer/winter seasonal terms.
  • Teaching and Research Support ProgramsA variety of teaching and research support programs, including a settlement fund of up to USD 27,200 for new faculty.
  • HousingUniversity housing near the campus provided for the first six months after appointment.
  • Additional Benefits for HCR-Level FacultyAdditional incentives, concurrent appointments at overseas institutions, and a reduced teaching load.
04 · BENEFITS

Benefits for Teaching-Focused Faculty

  • SalaryEquivalent to Hanyang University's Teaching-Focused faculty salary.
    An additional teaching allowance of up to USD 3,900 per semester is paid when teaching summer/winter seasonal terms.
  • Teaching Support ProgramsA variety of teaching support programs, including support for teaching methodologies and instructional development.
  • HousingUniversity housing near the campus provided for the first six months after appointment.
05 · BENEFITS

Notice on Visiting Professor Teaching Load and Compensation

  • RequirementsResponsible for 2 courses per semester (based on 3 credits each)
  • SalaryTeaching fees are paid differentially according to the number of courses taught (USD 7,700 for teaching 2 courses per semester / 4 courses per year)
    An additional teaching fee of up to USD 2,950 per semester is paid when teaching summer/winter seasonal terms.
